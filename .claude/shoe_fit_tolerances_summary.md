# Shoe Fit Tolerances — Summary Reference

*All values are clearance to add to foot measurements. Inputs: Foot Max Length, Foot Max Width, Toebox Max Length, Toebox Max Width.*

---

## Universal Rules

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Minimum toe clearance (any shoe) | **+0.39"** — below this, nail trauma is clinically likely |
| Toebox width compression | **Hard reject** — zero tolerance in every category |
| Fit to larger foot | Flag asymmetry >0.16" |
| Measure end of day | Feet swell +0.08"–0.16" length, +0.12"–0.20" width daily |

---

## Running

**Philosophy:** Roomy forefoot for splay and swelling; locked heel for friction prevention. Scale toe clearance with distance — marathon runners gain 4–8mm of foot length mid-race.

| Measurement | Min | Optimal | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foot Max Length → shoe length | +0.39" | +0.47"–0.59" | +0.79" |
| Foot Max Width → ball width | +0.00" | +0.12"–0.20" | +0.31" |
| Toebox Max Length → toe-tip space | 0.39" | 0.47"–0.59" | 0.79" |
| Toebox Max Width → per side | +0.12" | +0.16" | +0.31" |

**Heel slip:** <0.12" dynamic. **Distance modifiers:** Add 0.16" for half-marathon+, 0.24" for marathon+.

---

## Hiking / Trail

**Philosophy:** Descent toe protection is the primary concern. On steep grades with a loaded pack, the foot slides forward — insufficient clearance causes toenail damage with every step. LOWA's industry standard sets 0.47" as the hard minimum, measured on a decline with hiking socks and full pack.

| Measurement | Min | Optimal | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foot Max Length → shoe length | +0.47" | +0.47"–0.55" | +0.79" |
| Foot Max Width → ball width | +0.00" | +0.08"–0.16" | +0.24" |
| Toebox Max Length → toe-tip space | 0.47" | 0.47"–0.55" | 0.79" |
| Toebox Max Width → per side | +0.08" | +0.12" | +0.24" |

**Heel slip:** <0.12" broken-in. **Modifiers:** Thick socks +0.12"–0.20" to width; pack >55 lbs +0.20" to length; GTX membrane −0.06"–0.08" from available width.

---

## Training / Gym

**Philosophy:** Heel lockdown is stricter than any other category. Under loaded barbell movements, any heel movement transfers directly into form breakdown and force loss. Toe clearance is deliberately tighter than running — the foot is not repeatedly striking forward, so there is no swelling reserve needed. HIIT and plyometric sub-types allow slightly more forefoot freedom for jump landings.

| Measurement | Min | Optimal | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foot Max Length → shoe length | +0.39" | +0.47"–0.51" | +0.59" |
| Foot Max Width → ball width | +0.00" | +0.08"–0.16" | +0.24" |
| Toebox Max Length → toe-tip space | 0.39" | 0.47"–0.51" | 0.59" |
| Toebox Max Width → per side | +0.08" | +0.12"–0.16" | +0.24" |

**Heel slip:** 0.00" for lifting / <0.08" for HIIT cross-training. **Sub-types:** Olympic lifting shoes tighten to 0.39"–0.47" toe clearance and 0.00" heel slip; CrossFit/HIIT use upper range of optimal.

---

## Casual / Lifestyle

**Philosophy:** Width mismatch is the dominant failure mode — Buldt & Menz 2018 found 63–72% of people wear incorrectly fitted shoes, with width as the primary culprit. Diurnal swelling (feet expand throughout the day) must be built into the clearance. When between width sizes, always round wider.

| Measurement | Min | Optimal | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foot Max Length → shoe length | +0.39" | +0.47" | +0.59" |
| Foot Max Width → ball width | +0.00" | +0.08"–0.12" | +0.20" |
| Toebox Max Length → toe-tip space | 0.39" | 0.47" | 0.59" |
| Toebox Max Width → per side | +0.04" | +0.08"–0.12" | +0.20" |

**Heel slip:** <0.12" lace-up; 0.00" slip-ons. **Note:** Stiff uppers (leather, canvas) need full optimal; knit/mesh uppers tolerate minimum.

---

## Work / Safety (ASTM F2413 / EN 12568)

**Philosophy:** The rigid toe cap is a non-yielding wall — it does not stretch or break in. All width calculations must subtract the cap wall thickness before comparing to foot measurements. Shift workers also experience the highest occupational foot swelling (5.4% volume over 8 hours), comparable to marathon swelling, which must be buffered into length clearance.

| Measurement | Min | Optimal | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foot Max Length → space to cap interior | +0.24" | +0.39"–0.49" | +0.59" |
| Foot Max Width → width minus cap walls | +0.12"/side | +0.20"/side | +0.31"/side |
| Toebox Max Length → toe-tip to cap | 0.24" | 0.39"–0.49" | 0.59" |
| Toebox Max Width → per side (after cap) | +0.12" | +0.20" | +0.28" |

**ASTM post-impact residual:** 0.50" men / 0.47" women minimum. **Cap wall deduction:** Steel −0.06"–0.10"/side; Composite −0.12"–0.20"/side. **If toes touch cap walls, widen the shoe — never lengthen it.**

---

## Dress / Formal

**Philosophy:** Two unique variables. First, fashion geometry — pointed and chisel toe shapes extend well beyond the foot; this is non-functional length and must not be mistaken for toe clearance. Second, Goodyear welted (GYW) shoes have a cork midsole that compresses 0.04"–0.08" during break-in, so slight initial snugness is expected and acceptable. All other constructions fit exactly as purchased.

| Measurement | Min | Optimal | Max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foot Max Length → functional shoe length | +0.39" | +0.47"–0.59" | +0.67" |
| Foot Max Width → ball width | +0.00" | +0.08"–0.12" | +0.20" |
| Toebox Max Length → toe-tip to taper point | 0.39" | 0.47"–0.59" | 0.67" |
| Toebox Max Width → per side | +0.08" | +0.12" | +0.20" |

**Fashion allowance** (added to total shoe length; does not affect fit scoring): Round +0.00"–0.20" / Almond +0.20"–0.59" / Chisel +0.39"–1.00" / Pointed +0.59"–1.38"+. **Heel slip:** GYW new 0.00"–0.12", final 0.00"; Blake/Cemented 0.00" always.

## Algorithm Notes

- **Width mismatch is the #1 failure mode** — weight width 1.5× relative to length in scoring
- **Three hard rejects:** toebox width compression (any category), shoe shorter than foot + minimum clearance, toe contact with safety cap walls
- **Unmeasured dimensions** with no peer-reviewed standards: toebox height, midfoot snugness, heel width — use qualitative flags only
